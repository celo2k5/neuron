# NEURON — brag plan

**Tone:** polished (serious, elegant, restrained; long holds, soft dips through obsidian). The project is not a joke and the site was just made to look expensive, so the video should feel like a product film, not a meme.
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 21 s.

## Answers
- **What is it?** A neural-network lab in the browser: generate seeded networks, run them, train them, see them in six forms, and commit experiment hashes to Solana.
- **Who for?** People curious about how networks compute, and builders who want reproducible, verifiable experiments.
- **What sets it apart?** Every network is deterministic from its seed and drawn live from the real engine; experiments are hashed and the hashes committed on-chain (localnet in this build).
- **Most impressive claim:** the same network runs live in your browser and its experiment record re-verifies against the chain.
- **Visual hook:** the 3D specimen igniting as an activation pulse travels layer by layer through it.
- **Real UI to show:** home hero (headline rising), Lab (six forms, Execute), experiment page (Verified commitment), theme switcher.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Honesty guardrails (from the project spec)
- No mainnet, no invented token numbers. The commitment shown is on **localnet** and the caption says so.
- An on-chain hash is not called proof the computation was correct. The video says "hashes recompute", matching the app's own wording.
- The hero network is "Seeded, not trained", as the site says.
